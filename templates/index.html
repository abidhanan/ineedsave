Frequently asked questions

Is I Need Save free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, login, or app needed.

Where are my files saved?

On Android and computers, files go to the Downloads folder. On iPhone and iPad (Safari), they are saved in the Files app → Downloads. From there you can save them to Photos with the Share button.

Why can't some videos be downloaded?

Private content, content that requires a login, or content restricted by region can't be reached by our server. Make sure the post is public, then try again.

How do I download MP3?

After the link is processed, choose the MP3 option under Audio. The sound is taken from the video and converted to 192 kbps MP3.

Do TikTok videos have a watermark?

We always prefer the version without a watermark when it's available. Options that include a watermark are labeled Watermark.

Are my files stored on your server?

No. Files are sent from the original platform straight to your device and are not kept on our server.
