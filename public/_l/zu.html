Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe I Need Save imahhala?

Yebo, imahhala ngokuphelele. Akudingeki ukubhalisa, ukungena ngemvume, noma i-app.

Amafayela ami alondolozwa kuphi?

Ku-Android nakumakhompyutha, amafayela aya kufolda ethi Okulandiwe. Ku-iPhone naku-iPad (Safari), alondolozwa ku-app ethi Amafayela → Okulandiwe. Ukusuka lapho ungawalondoloza ku-Izithombe ngenkinobho ethi Yabelana.

Kungani amanye amavidiyo engakwazi ukulandwa?

Iseva yethu ayikwazi ukufinyelela okuqukethwe okuyimfihlo, okudinga ukungena ngemvume, noma okukhawulelwe ngokwesifunda. Qiniseka ukuthi okuthunyelwe kusesidlangalaleni, bese uzama futhi.

Ngiyilanda kanjani i-MP3?

Ngemuva kokuthi ilinki icutshunguliwe, khetha inketho ethi MP3 ngaphansi kokuthi Umsindo. Umsindo uthathwa evidiyweni bese uguqulelwa ku-MP3 ye-192 kbps.

Ingabe amavidiyo e-TikTok anophawu lwamanzi?

Sihlala sincamela inguqulo engenalo uphawu lwamanzi uma itholakala. Izinketho ezinophawu lwamanzi zibhalwe ukuthi Uphawu lwamanzi.

Ingabe amafayela ami agcinwa kuseva yenu?

Cha. Amafayela athunyelwa ngqo esuka enkundleni yokuqala aye kudivayisi yakho, futhi awagcinwa kuseva yethu.
