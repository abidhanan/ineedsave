Umgaqo-nkqubo wabucala

Iyasebenza ukusukela ngomhla wama-21 kuOkthobha 2026

I Need Save (ineedsave.com) sisixhobo sasimahla sokukhuphela iividiyo, iifoto nesandi kumaqonga onxibelelwano, esiqhutywa ngu-Abid Hanan Wicaksono. Lo mgaqo-nkqubo uchaza ukuba yeyiphi idatha esiyisebenzisayo xa usebenzisa le webhusayithi nendlela esiyiphatha ngayo. Ngabom siqokelela okuncinane kangangoko kunokwenzeka: akudingeki ubhalise, ungene, okanye unike igama lakho okanye i-imeyile yakho ukuze usebenzise inkonzo.

Idatha esiyisebenzisayo

  • Iilinki ozincamathiselayo. Ilinki ithunyelwa kwiseva yethu kuphela ukuze kufunyanwe imidiya enokukhutshelwa. Asizigcini iilinki kwisiseko sedatha kwaye asizidibanisi nobuni bakho.
  • Iifayile zemidiya. Nanini na kunokwenzeka, ibhrawuza yakho ikhuphela ifayile ngqo kwiiseva zeqonga lokuqala (umzekelo, Instagram, Facebook okanye X). Xa ifayile kufuneka isetyenzwe (umzekelo, ukudibanisa ividiyo nesandi okanye ukuyiguqulela kwi-MP3), okanye xa iqonga lingavumeli ukukhuphela ngqo, iqukuqela ngeseva yethu iye kwisixhobo sakho ngaphandle kokuba igcinwe.
  • Idatha yobugcisa. Njengaphantse yonke iwebhusayithi, umboneleli wethu wokubamba iwebhusayithi (Vercel) urekhoda idatha yobugcisa yezicelo, efana nedilesi ye-IP, uhlobo lwebhrawuza nexesha lokufikelela, ngenjongo yokhuseleko nokusebenza. Ezi rekhodi zigcinwa okwethutyana kuphela ngokwemigaqo-nkqubo ye-Vercel.
  • Iinkcukacha-manani zamanqaku. Amanani okujongwa, okuthandwa nokwabelwana agcinwa njengezimbuku zenqaku ngalinye, ngaphandle kwayo nayiphi na idatha enokukuchonga.

Iikhuki nendawo yokugcina kwibhrawuza

  • ins-lang: ikhumbula ulwimi olukhethileyo, kangangonyaka omnye.
  • dl_…: ikhuki yethutyana ehlala imizuzwana embalwa, ephawula ukuba ukukhuphela kuqalile.
  • Ugcino lwasekhaya (localStorage kunye ne-sessionStorage): lukhumbula amanqaku owathandileyo kwaye luthintela ukuba utyelelo olunye lubalwe kabini. Le datha ihlala kwisixhobo sakho.
  • Iikhuki zohlalutyo zamaqela esithathu ezivela kwi-Google Analytics nakwi-Microsoft Clarity, ezichazwe apha ngezantsi.

Uhlalutyo

Sisebenzisa izixhobo zohlalutyo ukuze siqonde indlela esetyenziswa ngayo iwebhusayithi nokuze siyiphucule:

  • Vercel Web Analytics: iinkcukacha-manani zotyelelo ezingachazi buni, ngaphandle kweekhuki.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): isebenzisa iikhuki ukulinganisa amaphepha owavulayo, ilizwe okulo ngokuqikelelwa, uhlobo lwesixhobo sakho, neziganeko ezifana nokucofa amaqhosha okukhuphela. Idatha isetyenzwa yi-Google ngokuhambisana noMgaqo-nkqubo wabucala we-Google.
  • Microsoft Clarity: ilayishwa xa uqala ukusebenzisana nephepha. I-Clarity irekhoda unxibelelwano olufana nokucofa nokuskrola ukwenzela iimephu zobushushu (heatmaps) neerekhodi zeeseshoni, kwaye ifihla oko ukuchwetheza kwiindawo zokufaka. Idatha isetyenzwa yi-Microsoft ngokuhambisana neNkcazo yabucala ye-Microsoft.

Ungaphuma kuhlalutyo ngokuvimba iikhuki zamaqela esithathu kwiisetingi zebhrawuza yakho, ngokusebenzisa isivimbi sokulandelela, okanye ngokufaka i-Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Iwebhusayithi iyaqhubeka isebenziseka ngokupheleleyo.

Iinkonzo zamaqela esithathu

  • Vercel Inc. ibonelela ngokubamba iwebhusayithi (hosting). Iiseva zayo zisenokuba ngaphandle kwelizwe lakho.
  • Google Fonts isetyenziselwa ukubonisa ezinye iindlela zokubhala (umzekelo, isiArabhu, isiThai okanye isiJapan). Xa ifonti ilayishwa, idilesi yakho ye-IP iyabonakala kwi-Google.
  • Iqonga lokuqala lemidiya. Xa ifayile ikhutshelwa ngqo, elo qonga linokuyibona idilesi yakho ye-IP, kanye njengoko kunjalo xa uvula umxholo ngokwakho.
  • Amaqhosha okwabelana (WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Telegram) aqhagamshelana nezo nkonzo kuphela xa uwacofa.

Ukhuseleko nokwabelana

Lonke unxibelelwano nale webhusayithi lusebenzisa i-HTTPS. Asiyithengisi, asiyiqeshisi, kwaye asiyirhwebi idatha yobuqu. Idatha yabelwana kuphela nababoneleli beenkonzo abakhankanywe ngasentla, kangangoko kufuneka ukuze kuqhutywe iwebhusayithi, okanye xa kufunwa ngumthetho.

Abantwana

Le nkonzo ayenzelwanga abantwana abangaphantsi kweminyaka eli-13, kwaye asiqokeleli ngabom idatha yobuqu evela kubantwana.

Amalungelo akho

Kuxhomekeke apho uhlala khona (umzekelo, phantsi koMthetho wase-Indonesia woKhuseleko lweDatha yoBuqu onguNombolo 27 wama-2022 okanye i-GDPR yoManyano lwaseYurophu), unokuba nelungelo lokufikelela, lokulungisa okanye lokucima idatha yakho yobuqu, nelokurhoxisa imvume yokuba isetyenzwe. Ngenxa yokuba asigcini phantse nayiphi na idatha enokukuchonga, uninzi lwezicelo lungasonjululwa ngokulula ngokucima iikhuki kwibhrawuza yakho. Kwidatha esetyenzwa yi-Google okanye yi-Microsoft, ungasebenzisa kwakhona iisetingi zabucala zeenkonzo zazo. Ngayo nayiphi na enye into, qhagamshelana nathi.

Utshintsho kulo mgaqo-nkqubo

Lo mgaqo-nkqubo unokuhlaziywa ngamaxesha athile. Utshintsho luqala ukusebenza xa lupapashwe kweli phepha, kwaye umhla wokuqala ukusebenza uboniswa phezulu.

Qhagamshelana nathi

Imibuzo malunga nalo mgaqo-nkqubo ingathunyelwa ngeelinki zoqhagamshelwano ezisezantsi kwiphepha ngalinye okanye nge-abidhanan.my.id.
