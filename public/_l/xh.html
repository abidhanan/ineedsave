Imibuzo ebuzwa rhoqo

Ngaba I Need Save isimahla?

Ewe, isimahla ngokupheleleyo. Akufuneki ubhalise, ungene, okanye ufake app.

Iifayile zam zigcinwa phi?

Kwi-Android nakwiikhompyutha, iifayile ziya kwifolda ethi Okukhutshelweyo. Kwi-iPhone nakwi-iPad (Safari), zigcinwa kwi-app ethi Iifayile → Okukhutshelweyo. Ukusuka apho ungazigcina kwiiFoto ngeqhosha elithi Yabelana.

Kutheni ezinye iividiyo zingakwazi ukukhutshelwa?

Iseva yethu ayinakufikelela kumxholo wabucala, umxholo ofuna ukungena, okanye umxholo othintelwe ngokommandla. Qinisekisa ukuba okuthunyelweyo kuvulelekile kuwonke-wonke, emva koko uzame kwakhona.

Ndiyikhuphela njani i-MP3?

Emva kokuba ilinki iqhutywe, khetha ukhetho oluthi MP3 phantsi kwesithi Isandi. Isandi sithathwa kwividiyo size siguqulelwe kwi-MP3 ye-192 kbps.

Ngaba iividiyo ze-TikTok zinophawu lwamanzi?

Sisoloko sikhetha inguqulelo engenalo uphawu lwamanzi xa ifumaneka. Ukhetho olunophawu lwamanzi lubhalwe ukuthi Uphawu lwamanzi.

Ngaba iifayile zam zigcinwa kwiseva yenu?

Hayi. Iifayile zithunyelwa ngqo zisuka kwiqonga lokuqala ziye kwisixhobo sakho, kwaye azigcinwa kwiseva yethu.
