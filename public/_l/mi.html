Ngā pātai auau

He kore utu a I Need Save?

Āe, he kore utu katoa. Kāore e hiahiatia he rēhitatanga, he takiuru, he taupānga rānei.

Ka tiakina ōku kōnae ki hea?

I Android me ngā rorohiko, ka haere ngā kōnae ki te kōpaki Ngā Tikiake. I iPhone me iPad (Safari), ka tiakina ki te taupānga Kōnae → Ngā Tikiake. Mai i reira ka taea e koe te tiaki ki Whakaahua mā te pātene Tiri.

He aha e kore ai e taea ētahi ataata te tikiake?

Kāore e taea e tō mātou tūmau te toro atu ki te ihirangi tūmataiti, ki te ihirangi e hiahia ana kia takiuru, ki te ihirangi kua herea ā-rohe rānei. Kia mōhio he tūmatanui te pou, kātahi ka ngana anō.

Me pēhea taku tikiake MP3?

Ka oti te hono te tukatuka, kōwhiria te kōwhiringa MP3 i raro i te Oro. Ka tangohia te oro i te ataata, ka hurihia hei MP3 192 kbps.

He tohu wai kei ngā ataata TikTok?

Ka hiahia tonu mātou ki te putanga kāore he tohu wai, ina wātea ana. Ko ngā kōwhiringa he tohu wai kei roto kua tapaina ki te Tohu wai.

Ka tiakina ōku kōnae ki tō koutou tūmau?

Kāo. Ka tukuna tika ngā kōnae mai i te pūhara taketake ki tō pūrere, ā, kāore e puritia ki tō mātou tūmau.
