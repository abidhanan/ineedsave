Me Pēhea te Tikiake Ataata mai i X (Twitter) mā te Māmā

15 Oketopa 2026

Ko X, i mōhiotia i mua ko Twitter, te wāhi tuatahi e puta ai te maha o ngā ataata rongonui: ngā wāhanga poto o te rongo kōrero, ngā wā hākinakina whakahirahira, tae atu ki ngā meme e kōrerotia nuitia ana. Ko te raru, ehara i te mea māmā i ngā wā katoa te tiaki i aua ataata ki tō Gallery mā te pēhi pātene kotahi. Kaua e māharahara, ka taea tonu e koe te tikiake ataata mai i X (Twitter) mā te māmā, mā I Need Save. He koreutu, kāore he taupānga, kāore he takiuru.

Ka kōrero tēnei aratohu mō ngā hipanga i te waea me te rorohiko, ngā kōwhiringa kōnae e wātea ana, me ngā rongoā mēnā ka rahua te tikiake.

I Mua i tō Tīmatanga

Kia mōhio nō tētahi pou tūmatanui te ataata e hiahia ana koe ki te tiaki. Kāore e taea te tikiake i ngā pou mai i ngā pūkete kua rakaina, i ngā ihirangi ka kitea noa i muri i te takiuru, i ngā ihirangi kua herea ki ētahi rohe anake rānei.

Kāore hoki koe e hiahia ki te tuku i te uru ki tō pūkete X. Ko te hono anake o te pou e hiahiatia ana e I Need Save.

Ka whai hua ēnei hipanga i te Android me te iPhone:

  1. Huakina te taupānga X, ka rapu i te pou kei roto te ataata e hiahia ana koe ki te tiaki.
  2. Pātōtia te pātene Share (Tiri) i te pou.
  3. Kōwhiria Copy link (Tārua hono).
  4. Huakina tō pūtirotiro, ka haere ki ineedsave.com.
  5. Whakapiria te hono ki te pouaka, pātōtia rānei te pātene Whakapiri.
  6. Pēhia Tikiake, ka tatari kia puta mai ngā kōwhiringa kōnae.
  7. Kōwhiria te kounga ataata e hiahia ana koe, ā, ka tīmata te tikiake.

I ētahi wā ka whakaatu te pātene i te E whakarite ana…. Ko te tikanga, kei te tukatukahia tuatahitia te ataata, hei tauira, kei te hurihia he rerenga (stream) hei MP4, kei te hono tahi rānei te ataata me te oro. Waiho kia tuwhera tonu te whārangi, ā, ka tīmata aunoa te tikiake ina rite te kōnae.

Me Pēhea te Tikiake Ataata X i te Rorohiko

Mēnā ka huaki koe i a X i te pūtirotiro o tō rorohiko, he māmā tonu te tikanga:

  1. Huakina te pou kei roto te ataata.
  2. Tāruatia tōna hono mai i te tahua Share, mai i te pae wāhitau (address bar) rānei.
  3. Huakina te whārangi tikiake i tētahi ripa hou, ka whakapiri i te hono.
  4. Pāwhiria Tikiake, kātahi ka kōwhiri i te kōwhiringa e hiahia ana koe.

Ka tiakina te kōnae ki te kōpaki Downloads (Ngā Tikiake) i te rorohiko.

Ngā Kōwhiringa ka Kitea e Koe

Ka oti te hono te tukatuka, ka whakaatu a I Need Save i ngā kōwhiringa e hāngai ana ki te pou taketake. I te nuinga o te wā ka kitea e koe:

  • Ataata i ētahi kounga, pērā i te 360p, te 720p, te 1080p rānei. Ko ngā taumira e wātea ana ka whakawhirinaki ki te ataata i tukuna.
  • Oro, i te kounga taketake, he MP3 192 kbps rānei kua hurihia mai i te ataata.
  • Whakaahua / Whakaahua iti mai i te pou.

He whai hua te kōwhiringa oro mēnā ko te reo anake tāu e hiahia ana, hei tauira, he wāhanga o tētahi uiuinga, he puoro rānei. Ka kitea ngā hipanga katoa i te tuhinga mō te huri ataata pāpāho pāpori hei MP3.

Kupu āwhina: Mēnā ko te mātakitaki anō i tō waea anake tāu e hiahia ana, he mārama tonu te kounga 720p i te nuinga o te wā, ā, he iti iho te rokiroki ka whakapaua i te 1080p.

He Aha e Whakamahi ai i I Need Save?

He take ētahi e pai ai tēnei tikanga mō ia rā:

  • Kāore he taupānga. Ka rere katoa i te pūtirotiro, nō reira kāore te rokiroki o tō waea e pau i tētahi atu taupānga.
  • Kāore he takiuru. Kāore koe e hiahia ki te waihanga pūkete, ki te hono rānei i tō pūkete X.
  • Ehara i a X anake. Ka taea te whakamahi i te tikanga kotahi mō TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, Bluesky me ētahi atu pūhara e tautokohia ana.
  • He pai ki te whakamahi. Kei te wātea te pae i ngā reo 93, ā, ka whai i te kaupapa mārama, pōuri rānei o tō pūrere.

Kei Hea te Kōnae i muri i te Tikiake

  • Android me te rorohiko: ka haere te kōnae ki te kōpaki Downloads (Ngā Tikiake). I Android, he wā poto pea i mua i te whakaaturanga mai i te taupānga Gallery.
  • iPhone/iPad (Safari): ka haere te kōnae ki te taupānga Files (Kōnae), ki te kōpaki Downloads. Hei nuku ki te taupānga Photos, huakina te ataata i Files, pātōtia Share, kātahi ka kōwhiri i Save Video.

He tika te roanga o ngā ataata MP4 kua tikiakehia, ā, ka tākaro i ngā taupānga Gallery. Ka tukuna tika hoki ngā kōnae mai i X ki tō pūrere, kāore e puritia ki te tūmau o I Need Save.

Mēnā ka Rahua te Tikiake

Anei ētahi o ngā take e kaha kitea ana:

  • Kua rakaina te pūkete. Kāore e taea te tiki i ngā ataata mai i ngā pūkete tūmataiti.
  • Me takiuru. Kāore hoki e taea te tikiake i ngā ihirangi ka kitea noa i muri i te takiuru.
  • Here ā-rohe. Kāore e wātea ngā ataata kua herea ki ētahi whenua anake.
  • Hono hē. Kia mōhio ko te hono o te pou tāu i tārua, ehara i te hono kōtaha. Tāruatia anō mai i te pātene Share.

Kaua e Wareware ki ngā Tikanga

Tiakina ngā ataata mō tō ake whakamahinga anake. Mēnā e hiahia ana koe ki te tiri anō i tētahi atu pūhara, tonoa he whakaaetanga i te kaihanga, ā, whakaingoatia ia. Mā te whakaute i te mana pupuri ka kaha tonu ngā kaihanga ki te auaha. Pānuitia hoki ngā tikanga me te mana pupuri mō te tikiake ihirangi mō tētahi whakamārama whānui ake.
